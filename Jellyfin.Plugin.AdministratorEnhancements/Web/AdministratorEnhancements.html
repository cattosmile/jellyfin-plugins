Jellyfin Administrator Enhancements

Seerr
The Seerr address reachable from the Jellyfin container. Docker installations normally use http://seerr:5055.
The saved key is shown as a password and is only used by Jellyfin to contact Seerr.

Deletion cleanup
When enabled, deleting a movie or series also removes its matching Radarr/Sonarr entry and Seerr request.
